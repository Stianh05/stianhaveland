Wipe Side is a mobile-friendly web application I developed for Egon while working there, with the goal of making it faster and easier for employees to restock refrigerators during busy shifts. The app contains a list of all products in alphabetical order. Users enter the number of units missing for each beverage, and the app automatically creates a picking list. The items are dynamically sorted based on their status, with active items displayed at the top, completed items moved to the bottom, and items with a quantity of 0 visually toned down. Once an item has been collected, it can be marked as completed by tapping the button in the top-left corner, and the list updates automatically.

I developed the project by combining Google Stitch for design and prototyping with Google AI Studio to build the functional application, before deploying and hosting it using Vercel. The project gave me practical experience in using AI as part of a modern development process, from identifying a specific problem in the workplace to designing, developing, testing, and improving a finished solution. It also gave me experience in how AI tools can be used effectively in programming and product development, while requiring me to evaluate and adapt the generated results to create a product that works well in a real-world work environment.
